Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

What the app accesses

Channel Dashboard asks for read-only access to the signed-in user's own YouTube channel (YouTube Data API, youtube.readonly) and its YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly). It reads channel and video statistics: subscriber counts, video titles, visibility, views, likes, comments and watch time.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the channel owner a summary of their own channels. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens and collected statistics are stored on the channel owner's own computer and in the owner's private dashboard. Nothing is stored by any other party.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local sign-in files removes all stored tokens.

Channel Dashboard's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
